# Commercialization hypothesis

This document records hypotheses, not validated demand.

## Target SMB customer

Initial target: small creator-led businesses, training/coaching companies, agencies, associations, and other SMB teams that publish video but want more control than a public social platform and less complexity than building a custom portal.

## Problem hypothesis

These teams may need a low-cost branded place to host/share video, organize content, control access, delegate publishing, understand engagement, and retain ownership of the workflow without stitching together multiple products.

## Value proposition hypothesis

**VideoShare gives an SMB a self-hostable video hub with creator workflows, controlled visibility, team publishing, analytics, playlists, Shorts, community features, and optional monetization while preserving deployment control.**

## Competitive frame

Alternatives include YouTube/unlisted YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Loom, course/community platforms, and custom website integrations. The project should not try to out-feature all of them. Pilot work should focus on a narrow buyer/problem where deployment control, branding, access control, or workflow ownership creates meaningful value.

## Pricing hypothesis

Do not treat these as validated prices. Candidate pilot packaging to test with real prospects:
- setup/pilot fee for deployment, branding, migration, and training;
- monthly support/hosting tier if managed hosting is later authorized;
- self-hosted/open-source edition as the adoption funnel.

No paid infrastructure should be provisioned before authorization.

## Validation plan

1. Build a concise SMB demo workflow around one target use case.
2. Conduct real buyer conversations and record the problem, current workaround, urgency, budget authority, and willingness to pilot.
3. Prefer a small paid pilot over adding broad features.
4. Add only features that remove a demonstrated blocker to a demo, pilot, retention, or supportability.
5. Record actual evidence separately; never convert assumptions into claimed validation.
